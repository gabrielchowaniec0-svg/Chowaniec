Questions

Good to know

Does the app store my data? Is it private?

The app is just chowaniec.net in its own window — nothing new is downloaded from an app store. Your messages and notes stay on the family server, behind the family passcode.

This device remembers that you're signed in (a small cookie and browser storage) so you don't have to type your passcode every time. If you turn on notifications, the server keeps an anonymous delivery address for this device so it can send you pings; turning notifications off deletes it.

On a shared or borrowed device, don't install it — or sign out when you're done.

How do I uninstall it?
  • iPhone / iPad: press and hold the Chowaniec icon, tap Remove App, then Delete from Home Screen.
  • Android: press and hold the icon, then drag it to Uninstall (or tap App info → Uninstall).
  • Computer: open the app, click the ⋮ menu in its title bar and choose Uninstall Chowaniec. On a Mac in Safari, delete it from the Applications folder.

You can still use chowaniec.net in your browser afterwards.

Notifications aren't arriving. What should I check?
  1. Use Send a test notification in Step 2 above. If it says it was sent but nothing shows up, the device is blocking it.
  2. iPhone: notifications only work in the app opened from your Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or newer), not in Safari. Also check Focus / Do Not Disturb, and Settings → Notifications → Chowaniec.
  3. Android: check Settings → Apps → Chrome (and Chowaniec) → Notifications, and that battery saver isn't putting Chrome to sleep.
  4. Computer: the browser must be allowed to show notifications in your system settings (macOS: System Settings → Notifications; Windows: Settings → System → Notifications).
  5. Still nothing? Turn notifications off and on again here — that gives this device a fresh delivery address.

You get a notification for each new message from someone else, and a "mentioned you" alert when someone @mentions you or @everyone. You never get notified about your own messages.

Can I install it on more than one device?

Yes. Install it on your phone, tablet and computer — just turn on notifications separately on each one.
